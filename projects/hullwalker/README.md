# HULLWALKER

**Status:** feature-complete, in playtesting; coming soon to Playgama (desktop and mobile browsers). **Genre:** retro sci-fi first-person walker-shooter.

![HULLWALKER: the Bridge deck](assets/project-cover.png)

## What is this game?

The last marine walks alone through the dead decks of a warship toward the bridge. Five decks stand in the way:
the Cargo Bay, Hydroponics, the Med Bay, the Reactor and finally the Bridge. Ambushes seal the doors behind you,
and they stay sealed until every Husk and Warden in the room is down.

You carry a two-handed shotgun that you work by hand after every shot, and a stock bash that shoves enemies back and
can knock Warden plasma bolts out of the air. It is my studio's first 3D game.

## How does it play?

- **Walk, stop, survive:** the marine moves forward on his own between fights, so the game plays well with one thumb
  on a phone as well as with a mouse.
- **Every shot counts:** the manual action after each shot turns reloading into part of the rhythm.
- **Parry the bolts:** a well-timed stock bash deflects plasma.
- **Three difficulty levels,** from Recruit to Nightmare, and a final siege with elite enemies.
- **Retro pixel-3D look** with its own music and sound.

## Where does it stand?

All five decks, enemies, weapons, music and sound are in. Automated test players get through all five decks, and
the Playgama build is packaged. It is now in hands-on playtesting, with the final choice of shotgun
still open.

## What comes next?

Finish playtesting, lock the weapon, then release on Playgama.
